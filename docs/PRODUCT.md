# CASP product overview

## The job

CASP helps people and AI systems discover stronger directions before committing
time, experiments, hires, or capital.

The user may already be asking the right question. The research system can still
return familiar products, obvious markets, or consensus-shaped recommendations
when the stronger direction depends on evidence outside the initial framing.
CASP is built to construct that missing direction, complete the research needed
to judge it, and make the next move testable.

## The operating loop

CASP performs one connected job:

1. Define the consequential decision, constraints, horizon, and attention limit.
2. Expand the candidate and evidence field beyond the visible frame.
3. Construct genuinely different directions rather than paraphrases.
4. Complete the evidence and counterevidence needed to judge each direction.
5. Concentrate limited attention on the strongest next move.
6. Attach a small test, uncertainty, and a condition for walking away.
7. Preserve the decision and observe what happened.

CASP can run when a person asks a research question, when an AI environment
reaches a consequential decision checkpoint, or continuously against a defined
opportunity field.

## Core, Overlay, and Enterprise

### CASP Core

The complete direct product for founders, developers, researchers, and smaller
teams. Core can use public sources, local files, approved models, and available
tools through a direct interface, supported AI environment, or API.

### CASP Overlay

The main early enterprise path. CASP begins with public evidence, a customer
brief, explicit qualification criteria, and optional customer-selected sources.
It can then incorporate approved exports and read-only connectors after value,
trust, and security review. Existing platforms remain useful sources and systems
of record while CASP supplies the cross-source discovery and decision loop.

### CASP Enterprise

The same system with private deployment or customer VPC, approved model
endpoints, governance, connectors, team workflows, continuous research, and
organization-specific decision and outcome memory.

## Model independence

CASP is not another foundation model. It is a governed research system that can
use different frontier models and retrieval providers. Model improvements raise
CASP's ceiling without replacing its workflow position, evaluation machinery,
or accumulated outcome history.
